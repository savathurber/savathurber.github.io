Geothermal Power Report
Environmental Science and Public Policy Final Project

A systems-level feasibility study for a 30 MW binary-cycle geothermal plant in Mary's River Basin, Nevada — combining geological resource assessment with nodal electricity-market analysis and a variable dispatch model.

Project Summary

How do energy developers quantitatively determine the economic viability of a geothermal power plant?

Mary's River Basin is a blind geothermal system in northeastern Nevada. Existing geological evidence suggests temperatures above 150°C at approximately 3 km depth, but the reservoir still requires exploratory drilling to confirm temperature amd permeability. The project determined whether the electricity could be sold profitably into the regional market while navigating local regulations.

Analytical Workflow for Economic Analysis

Overview of Workflow

The analysis sequentially considered upfront costs, operational expenses, and hourly electricity market dynamics for a comprehensive economic model. The model includes considerations for RECs and resource adequacy bonuses.

01 / RESOURCE

Resource

Evaluate temperature, reservoir architecture, permeability, and site constraints for a viable binary-cycle plant.

02 / PLANT

Generation

Model a 30 MW binary-cycle geothermal plant at variable (90%-98%) capacity factor.

03 / MARKET

Nodal Prices

Analyze hourly locational marginal prices (LMPs) across the market dataset collected from CAISO.

04 / DISPATCH

Optimize

Iterate across price thresholds to identify the economically optimal operating strategy.

05 / REVENUE

Stack Value

Combine electricity sales with REC and Resource Adequacy revenue.
